Corporate Carbon Footprint

Independent sustainability project | Carbon accounting | Excel

Project Overview

This project involved estimating the greenhouse gas emissions of a hypothetical London engineering consultancy using carbon accounting principles and emissions factors.

Objective

The aim was to create a structured carbon footprint model, identify the organisation's major sources of emissions and explore opportunities for reducing its environmental impact.

Method

I developed an Excel-based model using activity data, emissions factors and assumptions to estimate emissions across relevant Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 categories.

Key Outputs

The model summarises emissions by source and scope, helping identify the activities that contribute most to the organisation's footprint.

Skills Demonstrated

Carbon accounting, Excel modelling, data analysis, assumptions management, technical reporting and sustainability analysis.
